## Introduction

This paper presents a comprehensive analysis of modern optimization techniques for neural network training. We focus particularly on adaptive learning rate methods and their convergence properties.

## Background

Gradient descent optimization remains the cornerstone of neural network training. Recent developments have focused on adaptive methods that adjust learning rates based on gradient history.

## Methods

We evaluated several optimization algorithms including Adam, AdaGrad, and RMSprop across multiple benchmark datasets.

## Results

Our experiments show that Adam with β₁=0.9 and β₂=0.999 provides consistent performance across diverse architectures.

## Conclusion

Adaptive optimization methods continue to be essential for efficient neural network training, with Adam remaining a reliable default choice.
